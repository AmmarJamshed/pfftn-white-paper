# Limitations and Open Questions

* **Legal & liability** — Who is liable if a credential is wrong?
* **Data protection** — Consent, retention, cross-institution access under Pakistani law
* **Inclusion** — Feature phones, biometric exclusion, low literacy
* **Interop with Shared e-KYC** — Extension path, not a competing silo
* **Operational resilience** — Node failure, mass revocation, compromise response
* **Economics** — Who pays for verification without recreating friction?
